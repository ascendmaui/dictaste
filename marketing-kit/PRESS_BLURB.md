## About 100 words

Dictaste is system-wide AI dictation and highlight-to-speak for Mac and Windows. Hold a shortcut, speak, and polished text lands in the open app. Select text and hear it read aloud. On Mac, transcription uses on-device Apple Speech. Free includes 2,000 words of AI polish a day and system voices. Pro is $4/mo or $32/yr. Pro Plus is $8/mo or $64/yr. Developers who star johnmatveyev-lab/dictaste and bring their own API key use it free. The key stays on the machine. The primary download is the notarized Mac DMG, v0.1.4. Windows is an unsigned portable preview, and SmartScreen may warn. https://dictaste.vercel.app

## Boilerplate

Dictaste makes desktop dictation and highlight-to-speak for Mac and Windows. Mac speech recognition runs on device. Plans are Free, Pro ($4/mo or $32/yr), and Pro Plus ($8/mo or $64/yr). A Developer tier is free with a GitHub star on johnmatveyev-lab/dictaste and the developer’s own API key. Affiliates earn 30% of the first attributed Pro or Pro Plus invoice. The macOS and Windows clients are published under the Apache License 2.0. The hosted site, billing, and managed polish service are proprietary. Support: https://github.com/johnmatveyev-lab/dictaste/issues.
