## LinkedIn

Speak it. Ship it.

Dictaste is system-wide AI dictation and highlight-to-speak for Mac and Windows. Hold a shortcut, speak, and polished text is inserted into the focused app. Select text and hear it read back.

On Mac, transcription uses on-device Apple Speech. Windows can use free Web Speech or your own Whisper. Cloud polish sends text, not audio, when you use a cloud model.

Free includes unlimited system-wide dictation, 2,000 words of AI polish a day, and highlight-to-speak with system voices. Pro is $4/mo or $32/yr. Pro Plus is $8/mo or $64/yr. Developers who star johnmatveyev-lab/dictaste and bring their own API key use it free. The key stays on the machine.

The primary download is the notarized Mac DMG, v0.1.4. The Windows build is an unsigned portable preview, and SmartScreen may warn.

https://dictaste.vercel.app
