## Subhead

## Features

### Hold to speak

Dictaste is system-wide. On Mac, hold fn. Speech recognition on Mac uses on-device Apple Speech, so the voice stays on the machine. Polished text is inserted into the focused field: Cursor, Slack, Mail, Notes, a browser, Terminal, VS Code, or Claude Code.

Windows can use free Web Speech or your own Whisper. The default Windows polish-selection shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+P. Hotkeys can be remapped.

Requires macOS 13 or later, a microphone, and Accessibility permission. Speech Recognition is also required on Mac.

### AI polish

Polish turns spoken or selected text into cleaner writing: filler removed, grammar tightened, and multi-part thoughts broken into lines you can send.

The Free plan includes 2,000 words of AI polish a day. Pro and Pro Plus raise the managed polish limit. The published Pro and Pro Plus pages do not list a higher word cap, so do not invent one. Developer accounts use their own API key instead of the managed allowance.

When a cloud model is used, Dictaste sends text. It does not send audio for that polish step.

### Highlight to speak

Select text in any desktop app and Dictaste reads it. On Mac, playback can start when you finish selecting. On Windows, the default read shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+R. Both shortcuts can be remapped.

Free and the system-voice path use on-device system voices. Pro includes premium voices at 50,000 characters a month. Pro Plus includes premium voices at 200,000 characters a month. Premium voices can be OpenAI, Gemini, or Grok when you upgrade or bring a key. Space pauses. Esc stops.

### Always available on the desktop

Optional launch at login. Custom hotkeys. A menu-bar or tray control, including a reader bar for play, pause, speed, voice, and stop.

### What it does not do yet

Browser and mobile are later. There is no iPhone app. The 1290×2796 file in this kit is a vertical store-screenshot template for the desktop product.

## FAQ

### What is Dictaste?

Dictaste is a system-wide AI dictation app for Mac and Windows. Hold a shortcut, speak, and polished text is inserted into the focused app. It also reads selected text aloud.

### Where is the site?

https://dictaste.vercel.app. `dictaste.com` is not live.

### Does the free plan work?

Yes. Unlimited system-wide dictation, 2,000 words of AI polish a day, and highlight-to-speak with system voices. Upgrade for more managed polish or premium voices.

### Is it free for developers?

Yes. Star johnmatveyev-lab/dictaste, sign in with GitHub, install the app, and add your own API key. That Developer tier is free. The key stays on your computer.

### What does it cost otherwise?

Pro is $4/mo or $32/yr. Pro Plus is $8/mo or $64/yr.

### Which download should I use?

The notarized Mac DMG v0.1.4 is the primary download. Apple Silicon and Intel builds are both on that release. Windows is an unsigned portable preview. SmartScreen may warn.

### Does my voice leave the Mac?

Mac transcription uses on-device Apple Speech. Managed polish sends text, not audio, when a cloud model is used. You can also bring your own key.

### How does highlight-to-speak work?

Highlight text. On Mac, playback can start when you finish selecting. On Windows, press the read hotkey (default Ctrl+Shift+R). Free uses system voices. Pro and a bring-your-own key can use OpenAI, Gemini, and Grok voices.
